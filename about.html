About me

Meet the person behind Spooky Gaming.

Behind the screen

Who's Spooky?

Hey, I'm Spooky. Outside of gaming, I'm a husband, a dad, and a full-time field service technician. I spend most of my workday fixing equipment, and when I get home, gaming is one of my favorite ways to unwind.

What I play

I've never been able to stick to just one game. One night I'll be running a farm or driving a semi, and the next I'll be getting into trouble in an extraction shooter. I'm a sucker for a good simulator, but I'll give just about anything a shot if it looks fun.

Why I stream

Half the fun of gaming for me is having someone to share it with. I enjoy talking with people, hanging out, and seeing where the night takes us. That's what I want Spooky Gaming to be about.

What to expect

I'm not a pro gamer, and I'm not going to pretend to be one. There'll be good plays, bad decisions, plenty of laughs, and probably a few moments where I have absolutely no idea what I'm doing. Whether you're talking in chat or just hanging out in the background, there's a spot for you here.
